Mental health care has gaps.Reeboot is designed to live inside them.

Reeboot is a voice-led conversation the person opens on iOS, Android or the web. It is built in Australia by an AHPRA Registered Psychologist and Medtech Engineer. It sits with the person when the clinic is closed, and leaves clinical responsibility where it belongs.

The Clinician provides care.
Reeboot supports the person around it. Voice Led. Non Diagnostic. Risk owned by the product. Medmate for a Doctor, on the person’s terms. No conversation feed to the GP or the Psychologist.

The problem

What we are solving

The gap is not inside the clinic.It is before entry, between contacts, and after exit.

Reeboot is an AI-powered mental health platform that bridges the gap between healthcare appointments.

Today, mental healthcare is largely appointment-based. But people experience stress, anxiety and emotional difficulties every day, not just when they see a Clinician.

Reeboot changes that.

Built with a real-world clinical lens, Reeboot provides 24/7 personalised mental health support through natural voice and text conversations, drawing on evidence-based psychological approaches to help people manage everyday emotional and psychological challenges.

What makes Reeboot different is its connection to real healthcare.

Through our clinical partnership with Medmate, a national telehealth GP service, users can access on-demand virtual consultations with Medmate Doctors when their needs extend beyond digital support and require primary healthcare input.

Reeboot also incorporates clinical risk identification and escalation logic, designed to recognise indicators of significant psychological distress and direct users towards appropriate emergency care when required.

Our model connects three critical stages of mental healthcare: before care, between care and after care, supporting early intervention, continuity and ongoing wellbeing.

We're building the digital front door to mental healthcare: combining accessible AI support, real-world clinical pathways and scalable integration with Australia's primary healthcare through GPs, pharmacies and healthcare partners.

The three intervals

Before care. Between care. Continued care.The hours Reeboot is built to occupy.

01

Before care

Preventative access, with no referral required. The Australian median delay from onset of an anxiety disorder to first treatment contact is eleven years. Mood disorders: three years. Substance use: eight. In that interval the person is already unwell, and already talking. Reeboot is a clinically guarded conversation before a problem has a name.

02

Between care

Clinician Triggered, while the person waits. A referral can be written today. The first session may still be weeks or months away. In primary care psychotherapy, one in five dropouts occur between assessment and session one. Reeboot is offered alongside the referral, not instead of it, so that interval is not empty.

03

Continued care

Engagement the Clinician does not have to run. About a quarter of people leave psychotherapy before the work can hold. Completers average 7.4 sessions; those who leave, 2.4. Reeboot stays available between appointments and after an episode steps down, without turning routine use into a Clinician inbox.

The wait may be unavoidable. Unsupported waiting does not have to be. The interval Reeboot occupies

Health systems are still arguing about whether artificial intelligence belongs in mental health. The useful question is not whether a model will be in the conversation. It is whether the model sitting there is accountable to a Clinician, or to no one. The question the product is built to answer

What Reeboot is

A conversation the person can open without a referral.Voice or text. iOS, Android, and the web.

The person talks. Reeboot holds a clinically governed session: reflection, coping tools, journalling and continuity. Language is generated by a model. Safety, state, consent, escalation and session behaviour belong to Reeboot. There is no Clinician portal, and no conversation feed to the GP or the Psychologist.

Reeboot is a digital mental health support platform designed to complement, not replace, professional clinical care. It does not provide crisis intervention, diagnose conditions, make clinical decisions on a person’s behalf, monitor individuals outside active sessions, or function as a separate medical record.

Conversations may incorporate principles and techniques informed by Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), distress tolerance, family systems approaches and psychoeducation. These are used to support reflection, emotional understanding, coping skills and self-management, without representing the delivery of formal psychological treatment.

Between Care model · unchanged

Mental healthcare happens in appointments.Life happens between them.

Reeboot supports continuity from referral into real care: available immediately after referral, while a patient waits, and between appointments without replacing the GP, Psychologist or therapeutic relationship.

01

GP identifies need

The GP assesses the patient and makes the ordinary psychology referral. Reeboot is offered alongside the referral, not instead of it.

02

Reeboot begins immediately

The patient can use the Reeboot they already know while waiting: conversational support, reflection, coping tools, journalling and established safety pathways.

03

Psychologist provides real care

The Psychologist receives and manages the conventional referral. There is no Reeboot monitoring dashboard and no automatic activity feed.

04

Reeboot remains available

Between appointments, the patient can continue using Reeboot independently without turning routine platform use into Clinician-managed digital treatment.

05

The patient decides what returns

If a reflection, journal entry, coping plan or conversation is useful, the patient can choose to bring it into the next consultation.

Human care remains primary.Diagnosis, formulation, treatment and prescribing remain with Qualified Professionals.
Privacy is structural.Reeboot supports the patient. It does not report on the patient as part of this model.
Product discipline matters.The strategy uses the existing Reeboot patient experience rather than creating another clinical platform.
